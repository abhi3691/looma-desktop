Looma 0.3.4 saves wake greetings and voice replies in local conversation history, and updates the workspace when new speech exchanges arrive. History remains local under the existing questions-only cloud preference.

Voice interruption reacts to sustained nearby speech, keeps a longer audio pre-roll, and returns to listening after the interrupted response. Calibration uses quiet frames rather than the loudest opening sound.

The macOS installer includes the speech recognition models and standalone recognizer, plus the neural speaker executable. The online speaker requires internet access. Microphone permission is required. The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized.
